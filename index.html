Privacy Policy — Daily Record

Effective date: November 25, 2026

Daily Record ("the App", "we", "us") is a personal notes and daily-planning application. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, where it is stored, and your choices. It applies to the Daily Record mobile applications published on Google Play and the Apple App Store.

1. Information We Collect

Account information. To use cloud sync you create an account with an email address and a password, or sign in with a supported third-party provider (Google, Microsoft, Apple, Facebook, Kakao, or LINE, where enabled). We receive your email address and, for third-party sign-in, the basic profile identifier the provider shares. We do not receive your third-party password.

Your content. Notes, tags, daily plans, and daily summaries that you create. This content is stored locally on your device and, if you use cloud sync, on our backend so it can sync across your devices.

Technical data. Standard server logs (such as request timestamps and error codes) needed to operate and secure the service. We do not use advertising identifiers, and we do not run third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

What we do NOT collect. We do not collect your contacts, precise location, photos, microphone audio, or camera data. The App does not request those permissions.

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide the App's features: saving notes and plans, scoring your day, and syncing your content across your devices.
  • To authenticate you and keep your account secure.
  • To respond to support requests and to comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your content for advertising or profiling.

3. Where Data Is Stored (International Transfers)

Cloud data is hosted on Alibaba Cloud (Singapore region) using a managed PostgreSQL/Supabase service. If you access the App from outside Singapore, your data is transferred to and processed in Singapore. Where required by applicable law (for example, the EU/UK GDPR), such transfers rely on appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses. By creating a cloud account you consent to this processing. If you prefer not to transfer data, you can use the App's offline account mode, which keeps all data on your device only.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Your content is retained until you delete it or delete your account.
  • Account deletion: Settings → "Delete cloud account & data" permanently removes your account and all server-side records. This is available in the App at all times.
  • Local data can be erased at any time via Settings → "Erase all local data".
  • You may export a JSON backup of your content at any time via Settings → "Export JSON backup".

5. Security

Data in transit is protected with HTTPS/TLS. Access to cloud records is restricted by row-level security so that each account can only read and write its own data. Passwords are handled by the authentication service and are never stored by the App in plain text. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your personal information, and to object to or restrict certain processing. You can exercise most of these rights directly in the App (export, delete content, delete account). For other requests, contact us at the address below. We will respond within the time required by applicable law.

8. Third-Party Services

The App interoperates with: Alibaba Cloud (hosting), and — only if you choose to use them — sign-in providers (Google, Microsoft, Apple, Facebook, Kakao, LINE) and calendar services (Google Calendar, Outlook, iCloud, CalDAV) via standard .ics export. Your use of those services is governed by their own privacy policies. Calendar export is a one-way file export initiated by you; the App does not read your calendars.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy. The "Effective date" above will change, and material changes will be notified in the App or on the store listing. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.
